## **Testing *Transformations* Evaluation Criteria** 

**Venue:** Centre Marc Bloch, Berlin - Germany

This workshop is designed as part of the [ATRIUM project](https://www.atrium-research.eu/) - 7.4 task on “Implementing a Peer Review Framework” (see version 1, [https://zenodo.org/records/17787916](https://zenodo.org/records/17787916))  which main goal is to develop a qualitative, criteria-based assessment model in particular for [*Transformations. A DARIAH Journal*](https://transformations.episciences.org/) to assess and recognise, within the research evaluation landscape, critical research contributions such as datasets, models, workflows or software. This workshop is complementing a DARIAH Campus course on improving peer review skills with APRF  (to take place in Berlin, June 2027) and will enable the *Transformations* Editorial Board to improve their current evaluation grids and get feedback from the pool of *Transformations*’ reviewers for the new grids to be launched in Autumn 2026. It will provide participants (internal reviewers from the Scientific Committee board and external reviewers ) with an opportunity to discuss and share their reviewing methodology collaboratively and discover innovative evaluation processes for non-traditional outputs.
