ATRIUM successfully presented two research papers at the biennial Digital Humanities Congress 2026, held **September 2–3** at **the University of Sheffield.**&#x20;

Sarah Middle and Émilie Pagé-Perron presented ‘Enhancing the ARIADNE Portal through European collaboration’ and Diana Maynard presented ‘Archaeologists Only Need Small Maps: A Geometric Approach to Geoparsing’.

During the conference, ATRIUM researchers demonstrated technical enhancements to the ARIADNE Portal, including 3D visualization tools and expanded cataloging capabilities driven by EU-funded collaborations.&#x20;

Additionally, project partners presented a geometric approach to geoparsing designed to extract and disambiguate location data from archaeological literature

Both presentations highlighted ATRIUM's commitment to improving data findability, spatial analysis, and cross-disciplinary access for arts and humanities researchers across Europe. \
